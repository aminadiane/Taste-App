# Pallas — photography brief for the literature book

Use this brief to find or generate the reference photographs for the Pallas literature screen. The images will be cut out and combined with type and animation set in code, so the photographs must be clean, consistent, and free of any printed text.

## The project, in one paragraph

Pallas is an interactive experience by Cultural Capital that helps people develop their appreciation of literature and cinema. Its visual principle is: contemporary environment, nostalgic physical objects, tactile interactions. Each screen is stark white and minimal, with one hyperrealistic vintage object in the centre, photographed as if for a high-end catalogue or museum archive. The literature screen shows a single mid-20th-century paperback. The user opens it, reads a passage, and dog-ears pages they like or tears out pages they dislike. The cinema screen already uses a studio photograph of a silver early-2000s CRT television on seamless white; the book must look like it came from the same photo shoot.

## The object

A mass-market or trade paperback from roughly 1955–1965, in the spirit of early editions of Philip Roth's *Goodbye, Columbus* (Meridian / Bantam) and Françoise Sagan's *Bonjour Tristesse* (Dutton / Pocket). It must look like a real, used paperback, not a hardcover, not leather-bound, not academic, not a prop.

- **Format:** slim paperback, about 4.25 × 7 in (108 × 178 mm), around 160–220 pages, spine about 12–16 mm thick. Proportions about 1 : 1.6.
- **Cover:** a single solid flat colour, printed with NO text, NO illustration, NO photo, NO logo, NO publisher mark, NO price, NO barcode. A deep violet / indigo (close to #4A3E9E) is ideal. Any solid mid-to-dark colour also works, because the colour can be shifted in post. A slightly matte or satin coated stock, the kind used on 1950s–60s paperbacks.
- **Spine:** the same solid colour, blank, with a softly rounded spine and a gentle reading crease or two running vertically.
- **Page block (fore-edge, top and bottom edges):** visible, slightly yellowed with age (warm ivory to pale tan, not white), with natural page-edge striations. Faint foxing or toning is welcome; no staining.
- **Signs of age and handling (subtle, believable):**
  - softly rounded and slightly whitened corners, with the top-right and bottom-right corners a little more worn;
  - light edge wear along the cover where the colour has rubbed through to the paper underneath;
  - one or two faint vertical spine creases;
  - a very slight bow or curl to the cover, as a real paperback has;
  - no tears, no stickers, no library stamps, no writing, no heavy damage.
- **Interior pages (for the open shot):** off-white / warm ivory uncoated book paper (approx. #F2ECDC), slightly toned towards the edges, with faint visible paper grain. Pages must be completely BLANK: no printed text, no page numbers, no headers.

## Photographic style (applies to every shot)

- **Background:** seamless pure white studio sweep (#FFFFFF), with no visible horizon line, texture or gradient.
- **Lighting:** soft, high-key studio lighting from a large diffused source above and slightly front-left (about 10–11 o'clock), with a gentle fill from the right. Highlights must stay readable; no blown-out areas on the cover. No coloured gels. Neutral daylight colour balance (5500K).
- **Shadow:** one soft, physically convincing grounding shadow directly beneath the book, darkest right at the contact line and fading out softly within a few centimetres. No hard drop shadow, and no reflection unless it is extremely faint.
- **Lens and perspective:** long lens (85–135 mm equivalent) to minimise distortion. The camera is level and square to the subject unless noted.
- **Focus:** the entire book sharp edge to edge (f/8–f/11); no shallow depth of field, no bokeh.
- **Quality:** ultra-high resolution, at least 4000 px on the long side; crisp micro-detail in the paper fibres and cover wear; no grain, no film filters, no vignette, no colour grading, no Instagram look.
- **Mood:** the clean, quiet, considered look of a design-museum archive or a premium product catalogue: object-first and contemporary, with the object clearly vintage.
- **Strictly avoid:** any text or typography anywhere, hands, props, tables, wood, fabric, plants, coffee cups, glasses, leather, gold foil, embossing, dust jackets, hardcovers, antique or academic-looking books, illustrated covers, dramatic or moody lighting, dark backgrounds, coloured backgrounds, gradients, motion blur, and AI-looking smoothness or plastic sheen.

## Shots needed (in priority order)

### 1. Closed book: front cover, straight on (most important)
- The book lies flat on the white sweep, front cover up.
- Shot from directly above (top-down, 90°), perfectly square, with the book centred and upright and the spine on the left.
- The spine edge and a sliver of the page block on the right should be just visible from the thickness.
- Leave generous white space on all sides (the book filling about 50–60% of the frame height).

### 2. Closed book: three-quarter view with spine
- The same book standing or lying with the cover angled about 20–25° away from the camera, so the front cover and the full spine are visible together.
- The camera is slightly above (about 15°).
- The spine is blank and evenly lit, with its rounded profile and creases visible.

### 3. Open book: two-page spread, from above (essential)
- The same book opened flat to its middle and photographed straight down (90°), centred and square.
- Both pages BLANK, with a natural curve rising from the gutter, a soft shadow in the gutter, and the stacked page block visible at the outer edges (more pages on one side is fine).
- The covers are visible as a thin violet edge around the page block.
- Even lighting across both pages, with no hotspots, so text can be laid on top later.

### 4. Open book: low three-quarter angle (nice to have)
- The same open spread shot from about 30° above the table, to show the page curvature and thickness in perspective.

### 5. Details (nice to have, for textures)
- a macro of the interior page paper (blank, evenly lit, flat), to tile as a texture;
- a macro of a worn cover corner showing the rubbed colour and the whitened edge;
- a macro of the page-block fore-edge showing the aged striations;
- **if possible:** a single blank page physically torn out near the spine, photographed flat on white, showing a real torn fibrous edge;
- **if possible:** a single blank page with its top-right corner folded down (a real dog-ear), photographed flat on white.

## Consistency rules

- All shots show the same physical book, with the same colour, the same wear, and the same lighting setup and direction.
- Deliver as PNG or high-quality JPEG; a transparent-background cut-out is welcome but not required.
- Note the actual dimensions of the book if known.
