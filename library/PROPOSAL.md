## 2. What the sources allow

- **FilmGrab** states its images are "presented under fair use, for education and reference purposes only" and gives no permission to reproduce. It is a research index only. The gallery numbers below refer to the order on each FilmGrab page when viewed on 18 Oct 2026.
- **Wikimedia Commons** holds no frames from the copyrighted films above, with three exceptions that are genuinely cleared:
  - *2001*: a production still of Poole adrift beside the pod, 3747×1772, CC BY-SA 4.0 via Touring Club Italiano (VRT ticket on file). Attribution required.
  - *Lawrence of Arabia*: the original trailer is public domain in the US (published 1962 without a copyright notice), uploaded at 1920×800, plus two extracted train-attack frames at 1600×670.
  - *2001*: the original trailer is PD-US for the same reason, but the upload is only 640×280.
- **Public-domain trailers** on Commons for other Top 250 films, with upload resolution: Dr. Strangelove 1792×1080 · 12 Angry Men 1800×1080 · Rear Window 1440×1080 · Sunset Boulevard 1440×1080 · Some Like It Hot 1802×1080 · Paths of Glory 1802×1080 · The Apartment 1920×820 · The Bridge on the River Kwai 1920×814 · A Clockwork Orange 852×477 · Vertigo 757×436 · Double Indemnity 744×568 · The Treasure of the Sierra Madre 652×438 · To Kill a Mockingbird 634×472. All carry the PD-US-no-notice (or not-renewed) rationale; the trailer, not the film, is what is public domain, and that status holds in the US only.
- **Public-domain films** in the Top 250 (US: published before 1931): Metropolis (123), The Kid (146), The Gold Rush (211), Sherlock Jr. (213), The General (219), The Passion of Joan of Arc (247). Commons has The Kid at 1440×1080 and The General at 960×720 (complete), scene stills from The General up to 1901×1523, The Gold Rush at 480×360 only, Sherlock Jr. at 400×300 only, and no frames of Metropolis or Joan of Arc. Note: the EU does not treat Chaplin's films as PD until 2048.
- **Studio Ghibli** publishes 50 official stills of Spirited Away at ghibli.jp/works/chihiro (files chihiro001–050.jpg) with the statement 常識の範囲でご自由にお使いください ("use freely within the bounds of common sense"). Press coverage reads this as non-commercial. It is the strongest official permission among the ten films but it is not a formal licence.
- **Studio press sites** (Searchlight, Warner, Paramount, Universal, Neon, UGC/Miramax, Jet Tone) license stills for editorial coverage, not for integration into an app. Using them here needs a written permission request.

## 4. How it would be built (design untouched)

- A JSON library at `library/stills.json`: one record per still with film title, director, year, filename, source URL, rights status, colour palette, lighting, composition, framing, aspect ratio, and notes. Films are keyed by slug so the same file can hold hundreds of films with several stills each.
- Image files at `library/stills/<film-slug>/<nn>.webp`, resized to 1600 px on the long side for the screen, with the original kept in `library/originals/`.
- The television loads the library instead of waiting for a drop: the slot behaviour stays, so you can still drop a still over any library image.
- Aspect ratio: the prototype's slot crops to fill (`cover`). For cinematography that discards the composition, so I propose `contain`: the frame sits letterboxed on the dark CRT glass, exactly as a widescreen film looks on a 4:3 set. This is a CSS change inside the screen only; the set, layout, type, colours and animations stay as designed.

## 5. Decisions needed

1. Go ahead with the Tier A five-image test and the 31-still expansion.
2. For Tier B: request permissions, hold, or adopt a fair-use position.
3. Use the Ghibli stills under their stated terms, or ask Ghibli first.
4. `contain` (full frame, letterboxed) rather than `cover` (cropped) on the screen.

## 6. Decisions (20 October 2026)

- The project is non-commercial and will stay so. No permission requests go to studios.
- The copyrighted stills (In the Mood for Love, Amélie, Oldboy, The Grand Budapest Hotel, Mad Max: Fury Road, Parasite) stay in the library as they are, recorded as `fair-use` on an educational, non-commercial basis. Spirited Away stays under Ghibli's official release (`studio-release`).
- The open movies outside the IMDb Top 250 (Big Buck Bunny, Sintel, Tears of Steel, Spring, Sprite Fright, Cosmos Laundromat, Elephants Dream) were removed; the library keeps to Top 250 titles.
- Blade Runner, The Godfather and Schindler's List are skipped until a usable source exists. FilmGrab remains reference-only.
- Growth continues from public-domain Top 250 trailers and pre-1931 films on Wikimedia Commons.
